      Open in draw.io?

      The chart, including everything shown on the cards, is passed to draw.io inside the link. It is not sent to draw.io's servers, but the full link is saved in your browser history, and synced to the cloud if browser sync is on.

      For confidential data, download the .drawio file and open it in the draw.io desktop app instead.
